# Google Play visual assets

- `app-icon-512.png`: 512 × 512 app icon.
- `feature-graphic.png`: 1024 × 500, 24-bit PNG. The phone shown in the artwork is a real Tessarchy release-candidate capture.
- `screenshots/phone/`: real 1080 × 2160 Android emulator captures of the home screen, app library, and Studio.
- `screenshots/tablet/`: real 1280 × 1920 capture of the adaptive four-column home layout on a tablet-sized Android emulator profile.

The screenshot captures show the running app UI without promotional overlays. Keep the AI concept artwork in `docs/media/`; it is clearly labeled as future direction and is not used as a Play screenshot.
