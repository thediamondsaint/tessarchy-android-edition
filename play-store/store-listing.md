## Full description

Make your Android home screen feel like yours.

Tessarchy is a Metro-inspired home launcher built around a clear grid, expressive image-backed tiles, and a calm Tessarchy color palette. Set it as your Android Home app, then make the layout fit the way you use your phone.

**Shape your home**
• Choose Midnight, Daylight, or Cobalt themes.
• Reorder, resize, and hide optional tiles.
• Keep your most-used phone, messaging, calendar, music, and photo actions close.
• Use the searchable app library to find apps that Android exposes to launchers.
• Move between Home, Apps, Tessa, and Studio with the Window Tape.
• Return to your previous launcher whenever you want from Android Settings.

**Your choice, your app library**
The app library is optional. If you enable it, Tessarchy reads the names and icons of launcher apps from Android to display and search them on your phone. The list is kept in memory and is not uploaded, stored, shared, or used for advertising. Change this choice at any time in Studio → Privacy & data.

**A clear first release**
Tessa is the long-term assistant direction, and its screen is included as a preview. This release does not connect ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or another AI provider. Voice control, Android system replacement, rooted AOSP builds, third-party Android widgets, live app statistics, and side-by-side app windows are not included. Tessarchy is a launcher app, not an Android operating system or custom ROM.

No ads. No account. No analytics.
