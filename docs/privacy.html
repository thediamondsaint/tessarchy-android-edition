PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy describes Tessarchy Android Edition (TAE), a customizable Android home screen and app launcher. The current release has no user account, advertising, analytics, or AI provider connection.

Installed app names and icons

If you choose “Allow app library,” TAE asks Android for the names and icons of apps that expose a launcher activity. TAE uses that information to show and search the app drawer. It is held in memory while the launcher is open, refreshed when the launcher returns to the foreground, and cleared from memory when you turn app-library access off. TAE does not upload, store, sell, or share this app list. You can decline or turn off access in Personalize → Privacy & data. The app drawer is unavailable while access is off.

Launcher settings

TAE saves tile order, visibility, size, and color theme on your device using Android Preferences DataStore. Android device backup may include those settings when backup is enabled on your device. The app-list consent choice is stored separately and is not included in TAE’s backup rules.

Links to other apps

When you tap a tile, TAE may ask Android to open an installed app or content picker. Android and the app you select handle the resulting action under their own privacy policies. TAE does not read your calendar events, contacts, call history, messages, photos, or music library.

Network and third parties

The current release does not request internet access and does not send personal or device data to TAE, Tessarchy, or an AI provider. TAE includes AndroidX libraries and the Apache-2.0 Reorderable library; the app does not add analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

TAE is a general-purpose launcher and is not designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. Material changes to installed-app access, cloud services, or AI provider connections will be reflected in the app and in this policy before those features are offered.

For privacy questions, open an issue on the Tessarchy Android Edition GitHub repository.
